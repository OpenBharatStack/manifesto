# Governance — OpenBharatStack

> This movement belongs to no single person, company, or government.
> It belongs to the community — forever.

---

## Core Rules

### Rule 1 — No Single Owner
No individual or company can claim ownership 
of OpenBharatStack or its core protocols.

### Rule 2 — Open Standards Only
All core infrastructure must be based 
on open, documented standards.

### Rule 3 — Interoperability is Mandatory
Everything built under OpenBharatStack 
must work with everything else.

### Rule 4 — Public Auditability
All decisions, roadmaps, and changes 
must be publicly visible.

### Rule 5 — Data Portability Always
Users must always be able to export 
and move their data freely.

### Rule 6 — Forkability Protected
Anyone can fork any project here. 
This right can never be removed.

### Rule 7 — Distributed Governance
No single entity controls the roadmap. 
Community votes on major decisions.

---

## Who can contribute?

- Students
- Engineers
- Researchers
- Companies
- Government bodies

Everyone is welcome.
No one is in charge alone.

---

## Decision Making

- Small changes — maintainer approves
- Big changes — community discussion first
- Core rule changes — community vote required

---

## Code of Conduct

- Engineering excellence over politics
- Merit over identity
- Openness over secrecy
- Transparency always
